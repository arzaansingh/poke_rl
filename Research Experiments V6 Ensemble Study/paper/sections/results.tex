\subsection{Ensemble beats compute-matched single agent}
\label{sec:r_headline}

The headline result is \cref{fig:headline}. The $K=10$ soft-voting ensemble achieves
\textbf{66.8\% $\pm$ 0.3\%} win rate against SimpleHeuristicsPlayer over $100{,}000$
battles. The compute-matched single agent (baseline\_5m, trained for $5\times$ longer)
achieves only \textbf{54.2\% $\pm$ 0.3\%}, a lift of $+12.6$ percentage points
($z = 62$, $p < 0.001$). In direct head-to-head play, the ensemble wins
\textbf{59.7\%} of $100{,}000$ matches against the same single agent
($z = 61$ above coin flip).
